Université Grenoble Alpes

All of the lectures and courses listed below were taught in French, as part of the Bachelor's degree programme in Education and Training Sciences at Université Grenoble Alpes (France) between 2026 and 2027 (18 hours).

Methods and Tools for the Humanities and Social Sciences

Semester: first term
Public: BA 1st year in Educational and Training Sciences

To support and guide students through university life and the academic environment by providing them with the appropriate tools to succeed throughout their academic journey. The goal is to master academic writing, communication guidelines, explore note-taking methods, write a book report, conduct literature reviews, and learn about the experimental method.

References:

  • Guibert, P., Altet, M., & Maulini, O. (2021). Manuel de sciences de l'éducation et de la formation. De Boeck Supérieur.
  • Pougnet, D. (2024). Méthodo Rapido Réussir dans le Supérieur. BoD-Books on Demand.

Aix-Marseille Université

All of the lectures and courses listed below were taught as part of the Bachelor's degree programme in Psychology at Aix-Marseille Université (UFR ALLSH) between 2020 and 2025 (582 hours).

Academic Work Methods for Psychologists

Semester: first term
Public: BA 1st year in Psychology

To enable students to reflect on their work habits, organizational skills, and how to engage more effectively in various academic activities, and to help them improve their skills in researching, understanding, and utilizing information, as well as in note-taking and writing. The goal is to become proficient in AMU’s Digital Learning Environment (ENT), to understand the different types of academic exams, to explore methods of note-taking, to conduct bibliographic research, to analyze scientific information, and to produce academic papers and abstracts.

Reference: Piolat, A., & Vauclair, J. (2010). Réussir son premier cycle en psycho. Bruxelles : De Boeck.

Cognitive psychology

Semester: first term
Public: BA 1st year of Psychology

To introduce the main areas of cognitive psychology, from low-level processes to higher-level processes—that is, the full range of mental processes that enable us to understand the world, interact with it, and change it. The course will also aim to familiarize students with the experimental approach: developing analytical and critical thinking skills, understanding the basic principles of the experimental approach, and acquiring fundamental knowledge in the subdisciplines of cognitive psychology.

Reference: Lemaire, P., & Didierjean, A. (2018). Introduction à la psychologie cognitive. De Boeck Supérieur.

Semester: firs term
Public: BA 2nd year of Psychology

Introduction to the paradigms, concepts, and methods of cognitive psychology. The course presents the major cognitive functions (perception, memory, language, etc.), using these functions to introduce general paradigms in psychology. Particular attention is given to how psychologists interpret behavioral data and to cognitive interpretations based on psychological experiments. The objective is to become familiar with certain classic paradigms in cognitive psychology (priming, memory tasks, etc.), to interpret empirical data to draw conclusions about underlying psychological functions, to exercise critical thinking when encountering an experiment on a psychological function, and to design an experiment to answer a question related to one of the topics covered in class (conceiving the paradigm, developing the materials, collecting data, and writing a report).

Reference: Habib, M., Lavergne, L., & Caparos, S. (2018). Psychologie Cognitive - Cours, méthodologie, entraînement. Paris : Armand Colin.

Methodologies and Statistics

Semester: second term
Public: BA 1st year of Psychology

This section of the Methods and Statistics course (taught in the second semester) covers the z-score, the experimental method (variables, factors, experimental designs, analysis of main effects and interaction effects), and an inferential statistics test: the chi-square test. The objective is to understand the z-score, master the experimental method (identifying independent and dependent variables, designing an experimental design, and analyzing main effects and interaction effects), and calculate and understand the chi-square test.

Reference: Baggio, S. (2010). Introduction aux statistiques en psychologie. Méthodologie et analyse de données. De Boeck Université.

Semester: first term
Public: BA 2nd year of Psychology

Building on the content of the first-year undergraduate course, this course (offered in the first semester) aims to deepen students’ understanding of experimental designs and the conduct of statistical tests (inferential statistics) to compare means and analyze variances in the context of simple designs (with independent and repeated measures: t-test, ANOVA) and factorial designs (two-factor ANOVAs). The objective is to understand the logic behind testing the null hypothesis and to know how to apply it in simple and factorial experimental designs to compare means (t-tests) and analyze variances (ANOVAs) using a statistical software tool (e.g., JASP), to be able to (1) identify experimental designs, (2) determine which statistical tests to apply based on the hypotheses and experimental designs, (3) interpret the results of statistical tests in relation to a psychological hypothesis, and (4) visually represent data.

References:

  • Howell, D.C. (2008). Méthodes statistiques en sciences humaines (traduit par Y. Bestgen, M. Rogier, & V. Yzerbyt; 2nde édition). De Boeck.
  • Navarro, D., Foxcroft, D., & Meunier, J.-M. (2020). Apprentissage des statistiques avec Jamovi: Un tutoriel pour les étudiants en psychologie et autres débutants. [https://hal.science/hal-02335912v2/document](https://hal.science/hal-02335912v2/document)

Cognitive Neuropsychology and Psychopathology

Semester: second term
Public:BA 3rd year of Psychology

This course covers the historical foundations of neuropsychology, the basics of neuropsychological assessment, and the neuropsychological approach—from evaluation to treatment—as applied to various conditions (ASD, neurocognitive disorders, social cognition disorders, schizophrenia). The objective is to develop a comprehensive diagnostic approach, to formulate a hypothetical-deductive approach in the clinical setting, to make appropriate use of assessment methods, and to adopt an ethical perspective in both research and clinical practice.

References:

  • Amieva, H., Azouvi, P., Barbeau, E., & Collette, F. (2023). Traité de neuropsychologie clinique de l'adulte.
  • Eustache, F., Faure, S., & Desgranges, B. (2023). Manuel De Neuropsychologie - 6E Ed. Dunod.

Psychological Assessment Method

Semester: second term
Public:BA 3rd year of Psychology

This course covers the practice of psychological assessment (general principles, specific aspects, professional conduct, and the code of ethics for psychologists), third-party assessment (principles, methodologies, and tools), as well as the content of a test manual and its scientific validity.

References:

  • Castro, D. (2016). Pratique De L'examen Psychologique En Clinique Adulte - 3E Ed: Approches Intégratives. Dunod.
  • Grégoire, J. (2023). Evaluer l’intelligence : WPPSI-IV, WAIS, WISC-V, NEMI-3, WNV, RAVEN's 2, KABC-II et SON-R. De Boeck Supérieur.
